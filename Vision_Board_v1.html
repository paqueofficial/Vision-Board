Vision Board

A free canvas for capturing the feeling of a track or project — pin photos, notes and quotes anywhere and drag them around until it looks right.

How it works

  1. Name your board, then drop things onto it: Note for a scribble, Quote for a lyric or line, Text for a title, Photo for a reference image.
  2. Drag anything to move it, grab the corner to resize. Click to select; use the dots to recolour a note or the ✕ to remove it. Drag empty space to pan around the board.
  3. Double-tap text to edit it. You can also drag image files straight onto the board, or paste a copied image.
  4. Save a picture of the board (.png) to view it exactly as it looks — no internet needed, perfect for offline. Or save the editable board file (.json) to reopen and keep working anywhere. The page also auto-saves to this browser, but that's not a real backup.
