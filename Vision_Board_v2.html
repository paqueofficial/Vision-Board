Vision Board

A free canvas for capturing the feeling of a track or project — pin photos, notes and quotes anywhere and arrange them until it looks right.

How it works

  1. Name your board, then add things: Note for a scribble, Quote for a lyric, Text for a title, Photo for a reference. You can also drag image files straight on, or paste a copied image.
  2. Drag to move. Select an item to get its handles: the corner to enlarge or shrink, the top knob to rotate any direction. Note dots recolour; ✕ removes.
  3. Double-click text to edit — and click anywhere in it to place your cursor. The format bar lets you set bold, italic, underline, colour and a background.
  4. Snap (top bar) aligns items to the grid and to each other as you drag; turn it off to place things freely. Undo/redo step through every change.
  5. Save a picture (.png) to view the board offline, or the editable board file (.json) to reopen and keep working. The page also auto-saves to this browser.
